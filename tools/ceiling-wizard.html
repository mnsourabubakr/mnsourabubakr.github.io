Ceiling Wizard Tool

An automated suspended-ceiling design and modeling system that turns Revit rooms into calculated ceiling layouts — full gypsum, full tiles, whole-tile perimeter schemes, or divided bays — with height checks, room grouping, previews, and optional bulkheads.

What the tool does

Key capabilities

  • Four production workflows: Full Gypsum, Full Tiles, Perimeter + Whole Tiles, and Divided Bays
  • Reads compatible ceiling types and detects tile module dimensions from the bottom-side material’s orthogonal Model surface pattern for layout calculations
  • Checks proposed ceiling heights against the lowest detected structural soffit above the room, including floors, roofs, structural framing, foundations, and loaded structural links
  • Whole-tile perimeter design calculates tile counts and balanced clear borders within preferred, minimum, and maximum limits; equal ceiling heights suppress the vertical bulkhead automatically
  • Stepped perimeter layouts can create the center tile ceiling, gypsum perimeter, and optional vertical bulkhead walls as one coordinated Revit operation
  • Divided-Bay designer controls tile orientation, outer and internal gypsum bands, number of internal borders, equal or symmetric unequal spans, and final whole-tile counts with visual review
  • Groups rooms by compatible geometry and design basis so approved layouts can be reviewed and reused across similar rooms; host and linked rooms are supported
  • Creates and updates Ceiling Wizard-owned ceiling regions and bulkhead walls with worksharing checks and configurable handling of existing ceilings
Benchmark summary

Measured workflow impact

Ceiling Wizard combines ceiling-layout calculation and Revit modeling in one controlled workflow. Current V1 supports the four main patterns; Divided Bays is limited to rectangular rooms, and automatic alignment of the Revit material surface-pattern grid is not included yet.

Workflow impact depends on room geometry, existing model conditions, project standards, Revit version, hardware, and the selected design options.
