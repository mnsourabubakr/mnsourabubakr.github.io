Room Wizard Tool

A professional room-finish automation workspace that reads room data, detects existing floor finishes, configures single- or multi-layer finish build-ups, and safely creates or updates room-based finish floors with 3D review and conflict handling.

ArchitectureCeilings

Ceiling Wizard Tool

An automated suspended-ceiling design and modeling system that turns Revit rooms into calculated ceiling layouts — full gypsum, full tiles, whole-tile perimeter schemes, or divided bays — with height checks, room grouping, previews, and optional bulkheads.

Reads CAD layer geometry and automatically generates Revit foundation elements — isolated footings and raft slabs — with correct dimensions, types, naming conventions, and level offsets.
